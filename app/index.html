Intended research use

  • Highlight the brachial plexus on a single interscalene ultrasound frame.
  • Teaching sonoanatomy and benchmarking annotation.
  • Frames like the two training sites': interscalene view, the plexus in cross-section.

Known limits

  • 135 patients from two public datasets, one annotator each, noisy labels at site A.
  • Trained and tested at two hospitals only. An earlier single-site version found no nerve at all on the second hospital's scanner, so expect failure on a third machine until it is tested there.
  • Not tested on other block approaches or children.
  • Single frames only. No needle tracking, no live video.
  • Never use it to guide a real needle or a clinical decision.
